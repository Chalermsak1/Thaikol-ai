# ThaiKOL AI — Data Ethics, Privacy & Responsible AI Policy

This document outlines the ethical boundaries, privacy protections, and methodological limitations governing **ThaiKOL AI**.

---

## 1. Core Principles

### A. Exclusively Public Data
- ThaiKOL AI operates **strictly on publicly accessible web pages and social media content**.
- The system **never accesses**:
  - Private messages (DMs), closed group chats, or subscriber-only feeds.
  - Authenticated user account credentials, tokens, or sessions.
  - Non-public contact details (personal phone numbers, home addresses, private emails).
  - Private platform analytics dashboards requiring creator OAuth login.

### B. Anti-Fabrication & Demographic Integrity
- **Zero Demographic Fabrication**: Commercial platforms frequently fabricate or hallucinate follower demographics (e.g. age distributions, gender splits, household income, geographic coordinates) without actual access to internal platform databases.
- ThaiKOL AI rejects demographic hallucination:
  - The system **never displays or estimates unverifiable audience demographics**.
  - Where audience alignment is evaluated, it is explicitly titled **"Audience Fit Proxy"** and computed purely from observable public content signals (Thai language percentage, local Thai location mentions, niche hashtag frequency).
  - The Audience Fit Proxy is strictly informational and **does not alter or leak into the Final Recommendation Score**.

### C. Content-Level Screening vs. Personal Character
- **Brand Safety Definition**: Brand safety checks in ThaiKOL AI are strictly **content-level screening of public video captions and hashtags** for commercial campaign suitability (e.g. gambling keywords, prohibited supplements, unverified medical claims).
- **Not a Personal Judgment**: A brand safety flag represents an objective match against a configurable commercial keyword dictionary (`config/brand_safety.yaml`). It is **not a judgment about a creator's personal character, ethics, or reputation**.
- The user interface provides transparent evidence strings and deduction explanations for any caution raised.

## 2. Platform Sampling & Measurement Boundaries

### A. Point-in-Time Public Snapshots
- Social media metrics (likes, views, comments, shares, follower counts) fluctuate continuously.
- All metrics presented in ThaiKOL AI represent a **point-in-time snapshot** captured during discovery or derived from a curated assessment fixture.
- Every metric card and API response includes a `collected_at` ISO-8601 timestamp and explicit `provenance` metadata.

### B. Sampled vs. Exhaustive Data
- Public scrapers ingest a sample of recent videos (typically 10 to 30 videos per query) due to platform rate limits and latency constraints.
- Estimated Engagement Rates ($\text{ER}$) are calculated across the sampled videos, not the creator's lifetime history.
- The candidate pool does **not represent the entire population of TikTok creators in Thailand**, but rather relevant creators discoverable through specific, targeted search terms.

### C. Transparent Data Provenance (Live vs. Demo)
- To ensure complete operational transparency:
  - **Live Mode**: Labeled with `LIVE — Public TikTok Snapshot` only when metrics originate from active external provider runs.
  - **Demo Mode**: Labeled with `DEMO DATA — Curated Benchmark Fixture` when using curated offline benchmarks (e.g. the Khaokho Talaypu dataset). The system never masquerades offline fixtures as live platform data.

## 3. Responsible AI & System Limitations

| Potential Risk | Ethical Boundary in ThaiKOL AI | System Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **Hallucinated Recommendations** | LLMs are not allowed to invent fake influencers or fake stats. | Deterministic scoring; all creator candidates must exist in the database or fixture. |
| **Biased Scoring** | Opaque black-box models could unfairly penalize niche creators. | Fully transparent, audited 5-factor scoring formula with granular component breakdowns. |
| **Anti-Scraping / DDOS Risks** | Aggressive scraping can degrade public infrastructure. | Built-in request caching, exponential backoff, rate limits, and offline fallback mode. |
| **Data Retention** | Storing unnecessary personal information. | Only public usernames, public captions, and public aggregate engagement counts are cached. |

## 4. Methodological Disclaimer for Marketers

> [!NOTE]
